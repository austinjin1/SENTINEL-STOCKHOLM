# EW-v2 — Pre-registration protocol for SENTINEL freshwater hazard early warning

**Status:** DRAFT v0.3 (2026-11-08; v0.2 revised after the pre-test review "EW-v2 — pre-test / pre-registration gate (2026-11-08)", v0.3 after the re-review "EW-v2 — pre-test re-review (post final chain, 2026-11-08)" in REVIEW_REPORT_PRE_TRAINING.md; changes logged in §12). To be frozen (`docs/preregistration/FREEZE_ew.json`, hashes in §10) and registered (OSF) **before any Test-A (WY2024–2025) or Test-B data are loaded by any model-scoring code**; the 2026-11-15 target may slip, the order may not. The live prospective trial (former §11) is registered as a separate amendment before 2026-12-01.
**Authors:** Austin Jin, Bryan Cheng (AI-assisted). **Implements:** RESEARCH_PLAN.md §12.

## 1. Objective and hypotheses
For sensor-observable freshwater hazards, test whether SENTINEL forecasts of *hazard onset* within h days carry skill over a site × season climatology at **stations never used in training or calibration**, and whether alarms raised at a frozen false-alarm budget precede real onsets more often than season-matched placebo dates.

- **H-a (primary, per hazard in the family {H1, H2, H4}):** BSS of the selected SENTINEL method vs the identically recalibrated climatology at h = 3 d > 0 at test-role sites × Test-A (§7).
- **H-b (secondary):** Brier score lower than the probabilistic baselines persistence/current-state, logistic regression on lags and the event GBM (paired ΔBS, basin-group bootstrap; Diebold–Mariano). The threshold-on-current-value rule is not a probability; it is compared on POD at the frozen alarm budget only.
- **H-c (secondary):** POD before real onsets exceeds POD before season-matched placebo onsets (one-sided; basin-group cluster bootstrap of POD_real − POD_placebo; the Fisher test is descriptive only).

**Expected outcome stated before unblinding:** validation evidence is weak (H1 stack val-OOF BSS +0.022, basin-group CI [−0.015, 0.043]; H2 18 and H4 12 positive val rows) and H1 is projected to have ≈ 44 evaluable Test-A onsets at test-role sites (< 50, §9). The RESEARCH_PLAN §15.2 EW criterion (≥ 2 hazards pass on Test-A) is therefore **expected to fail**; the result is reported whatever it is.

## 2. Data and eligibility
- USGS continuous water-quality **daily values** (daily min/mean/max/median; `sentinel/data/sonde_daily.py`), hourly aggregates of the 15-min tier for the sub-daily DO features (`sentinel/models/sonde/subdaily.py`), gridMET lagged observed weather (H2/H4 only), WQP–SC chloride regressions (H2). Snapshots pinned in §10.
- **Labels use Approved values only** (provisional values are treated as missing for labels and placebo candidates). Model inputs use the values available in the pinned snapshot (approved and provisional, as an operational forecaster would see them).
- Missing-value codes masked at ingest; qualifiers e (estimated), <, >, [4] masked; physical plausibility ranges in `sonde_daily.PLAUSIBLE`.
- Issue-row validity (code: `hazards.issue_labels` with `refractory_days`): the issue day d is observed and is not an event day; a negative label (no onset in (d, d+h]) needs ≥ 80% of the days in (d, d+h] observed (positives exempt); rows where an onset in (d, d+h] is impossible by the refractory rule ("continuation" days) are invalid. The variable must be forecastable at d (finite last value and site climatology), identical rows for every method.
- Sites with < 365 training-block days (no climatology) are unscorable (reported, not imputed).
- The 15-min-tier event durations in the earlier draft are **not operational** in Test-A scoring (daily tier only).

## 3. Hazard definitions (criterion-anchored excursions; daily tier, exactly as `sentinel/eval/hazards.py`)

| Key | Variable | Event day | Clean pre-window before the onset day d (all days observed) | Refractory | Anchor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | DO daily min | < 3.0 mg/L | 3 days ≥ 4.0 mg/L, [d−3, d−1] | 7 d | EPA 1986 warmwater 1-day min |
| H1 tier < 4 / < 2 (secondary) | DO daily min | < 4.0 / < 2.0 mg/L | 3 days ≥ 5.0 / ≥ 3.0 mg/L | 7 d | sensitivity tiers |

## 4. Forecast task
- Daily issue at the end of day d (day-d statistics complete); inputs available at issue time only (lagged observed weather published by the end of day d; no observed future weather).
- Target: P(onset within (d, d + h]), h ∈ {1, 3, 7} days; primary h = 3.

## 5. Models compared (identical inputs, rows and splits)
Baselines: climatology (site × DOY harmonic logistic, pooled + ridge per-site deviations from each site's own training years; test-role sites receive deviations from their own training-block labels only), persistence/current-state, threshold on current value, logistic regression on lags, event GBM (5-seed bagged LightGBM). SENTINEL candidates: monotone event GBM, logistic stack of the feature-based methods (event GBM, monotone GBM, logistic, persistence, climatology; fitted on val). M1-forecast-derived exceedance methods (q_*) are not scored on Test-A in this registration (they need the separate M1 freeze); q_damped_anomaly (internal naive) is scored descriptively. **The selected EW method contains no M1 forecasting or foundation-model component**; claims describe it as a calibrated stack of statistical baselines plus a monotone GBM. v1 CUSUM: not re-scored (deviation 2026-11-08: the v1 detector runs on v1 AquaSSM residual caches that do not exist for these rows); v1 is reported as retracted.
**Selection (pre-specified, `scripts/eval/sonde_ew_freeze_candidate.py`):** per hazard, the candidate with the highest val out-of-fold (by water year) BSS at h = 3 d among non-baseline, non-q methods. **Configurations tried (val only): 8 calibrate configurations + 5 offline stack variants** (feat, feat+qda, feat+allq, gbm+clim, feat+qft; registry `ew_v2_stack_explore`). A 9th calibrate run (`ew_live_variant_cal_nosubdaily`, H1/H4 without sub-daily features, 2026-11-08 19:25) calibrates the **live-trial** provisional-input variant (§11); it ran after the candidate selection and is not used for any selection here.

## 6. Splits, calibration, freezing
- Train WY2008–2021; calibration/validation WY2022–2023; **Test-A WY2024–2025 (scored once)**; Test-B WY2026 (only in the pooled secondary, §9). 42-day embargo at block starts (issue dates).
- Spatial: basin groups (HUC4 merged across flow-connected stations), 70/10/20 train/val/test by sites; test-role basins never used in training or calibration (split §10).
- All fitting on train-role sites × train block (event GBM: d + L ≤ 2019-09-30, early stopping on WY2020–21 issued ≥ 2019-11-12). Platt recalibration (every probabilistic method incl. the climatology reference), the stack and the alarm thresholds are fitted on **all** val rows (all roles) and frozen; the out-of-fold-by-water-year versions are used only for the val descriptives.
- Alarm thresholds: alarm-days on valid non-onset issue days ≤ 1 per site per 30 days pooled (POFD ≤ 1/30; sensitivity 2/30); every alarmed day counts.
- Forecast quantiles clipped to `PLAUSIBLE`. Scope: stream sites (ST, ST-TS, ST-CA, ST-DCH).
- **Frozen invocation (review C2):** test scoring runs only `python -B scripts/train/sonde_events.py --phase score --config configs/sonde/events.yaml --calibration <frozen file> --eval-blocks test --allow-test --include-test-sites --freeze-file docs/preregistration/FREEZE_ew.json --run-name ew_v2_testA_score`; the code refuses `--set`, `--seed`, `--smoke`, other configs, quantile models or LSTM checkpoints not in the record, and verifies the config hash against the calibration file and the freeze record.
- **Single pass (review C3; amended v0.3, re-review C4):** an atomic marker with a **fixed name** `sentinel_runs/sonde/.ew_scored_<pass>.json` (independent of the freeze record, so re-writing `FREEZE_ew.json` can never re-open a pass) is claimed before any protected value is loaded, and a `pass_claimed` row of experiment `ew_v2_<pass>_score` is written to the results registry immediately afterwards; the primary endpoint and every numeric control of the pass are registered under the same experiment when scoring ends. A pass is refused if any marker of that pass exists (including legacy `…_<sha12>.json` names) or the registry holds any `ew_v2_<pass>_score` row. Every check (invocation incl. `--split` refusal and split-file hash, calibration hash, split, pinned data snapshots) runs before the marker is claimed, so a failed check never consumes the pass. **Data pins (re-review W1/C1):** for each loader directory (`sonde_daily`, `sonde_hourly`, `forcings_daily`, `chloride_derived`) the record pins the manifest SHA-256 and a **content hash** = SHA-256 over the sorted `<relative path>\t<file SHA-256>` lines of every file the loader reads (`sonde_events.SNAPSHOT_FILES`); the score phase recomputes it and refuses on any difference or missing pin. In addition the score phase refuses if the attached H2 chloride differs from `clip(a + b·SC, 0)` of the loaded SC cube on any day (`check_chloride_consistency`). The pooled secondary has its own pass name (`test_ab`) and may not run before its approval cut-off; its data pin is defined in §9.

## 7. Endpoints and success criteria
- **Primary population: test-role sites × Test-A (WY2024–25), Approved-value labels.** All-roles, train-role and val-role × Test-A cells are reported as labelled secondary cells (temporal-only generalisation).
- **Primary statistic:** pooled BSS of the selected method vs the climatology reference recalibrated by the same frozen Platt step as every other method, h = 3 d.
- **CI and test:** basin-group > site-month two-stage block bootstrap (B = 2,000); one-sided p = (1 + #{BSS* ≤ 0}) / (1 + B).
- **Multiplicity:** Holm over the fixed family {H1, H2, H4} (m = 3) whatever the onset counts; α = 0.05.
- **Evaluable onset (one definition; amended v0.3, re-review C2):** an onset of the primary cell is evaluable iff it is not censored, lies in (first issue date, last issue date + 3 d] and has ≥ 1 usable issue row (valid row of the cell with a finite forecast) in [onset − 3 d, onset). The same function (`ew_scoring.evaluable_onsets`) defines the ≥ 50-onset gate, POD, the placebo comparison and every alarm-series control.
- **Power rule (amended v0.3, re-review C3):** a hazard is "underpowered" when it has < 50 evaluable onsets at test-role sites in Test-A **or** < 2 basin groups with evaluable onsets (the outer bootstrap stage is then degenerate). An underpowered hazard (and a hazard without a result) **enters Holm with p = 1** (still counted in m = 3) and cannot be claimed; its bootstrap p is reported as descriptive only (`p_one_sided_descriptive`). (v0.2 let underpowered hazards enter Holm with their bootstrap p, which could spuriously step down H1's threshold.)
- **Success for a hazard:** not underpowered AND Holm rejection (with the p = 1 inputs above).
- **Reporting (re-review W4):** the number of basin groups with evaluable onsets is reported next to every primary p and CI; with < 10 such groups the two-stage CI is stated to be approximate.
- **Secondary:** paired ΔBS vs each probabilistic baseline (basin-group bootstrap) and Diebold–Mariano (daily site-mean Brier differential, Newey–West, lag 7); POD (≥ 24 h warning) at the frozen budget with realised alarm-days; POD_real − POD_placebo (cluster bootstrap); Molchan area skill; reliability table + ECE; relative economic value at C/L ∈ {0.01, 0.02, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.5}; tiers and H2-acute descriptive; pooled Test-A ∪ Test-B H1 (§9).
- Realised out-of-sample alarm rates are reported as they are (val OOF stack 1.03–1.05 alarm-days per 30 d); thresholds are never re-tuned. Discrete scorers (persistence, threshold) have ties that keep realised rates below budget; their budget-matched POD is reported with that caveat.
- **Claims allowed:** "skill at 3 days at unseen stations" only for hazards meeting the success rule; lead times quoted as the distribution of warning times of hits.

## 8. Controls (computed on the frozen test predictions of the selected method, primary cell, h = 3; `sentinel/eval/ew_controls.py`)
Season-matched placebo onsets (K = 20, clean candidate days, away from all events) with cluster-bootstrap test · random alarms at each site's realised alarm rate (analytic expected POD) · 1,000 circular-shift surrogates of the alarm series (≥ 30 d, independent per site) · label permutation within site (circular shift of each site's label series ≥ 30 rows, 1,000 draws; BSS null) · negative-control sites (H1 family: sites whose training-block DO min never < 6 mg/L): realised alarm-days and onset rows · covariate shuffle (non-target covariates permuted across sites within issue date; BSS) · input-truncation audit (features recomputed after removing all values after three issue dates; max feature difference must be 0) · Molchan, reliability, REV, Diebold–Mariano (§7).
Deviations (2026-11-08): **station swap** dropped (no pre-definable pairing of test-role sites that preserves climate and sensor type); **sensor-fault audit** deferred to 2027 (needs provisional-vs-approved snapshots of Test-A alarms; the 2026-11-08 snapshot and a post-approval snapshot will be compared); **v1 CUSUM** not re-scored (§5).

## 9. Sample size
≥ 50 evaluable onsets per hazard at test-role sites in Test-A (h = 3) required for a claim; otherwise "underpowered" (§7). Calibration-period onset counts: H2 (computed 2026-10-25 from training/validation blocks only, test block not inspected; docs/datasheets/chloride_derived.md): 52 eligible sites; WY2022–23 chronic onsets 1 at val-role sites (12 across all roles — this count includes test-role sites and was computed outside the guard by the chloride builder; disclosed in §12) → **H2 is underpowered for the Test-A claim and is expected to be reported as such**; its primary use moves to the live winter trial (2026-12-01 – 2027-03-31). **A-priori H2 Test-A projection (re-review W3, from train/val-role rates only; no test-block value used):** 52 eligible H2 sites by split role: 27 train, 9 val, 13 test, 3 not in split (eligibility uses training-block pairs only; the per-role eligibility count comes from the chloride build disclosed in §12 W11). WY2022–23 chronic onsets at train- and val-role sites: 5 + 1 = 6 over 36 sites × 2 WY = 0.083 onsets per site-WY → **≈ 2 projected chronic onsets at the 13 test-role sites in WY2024–25** (upper scenario at the WY2008–21 train/val-role rate, 134 onsets / 36 sites / 14 WY = 0.27 per site-WY: ≈ 7), in at most a handful of basin groups. **H2 is therefore evaluated only descriptively on Test-A** (it enters Holm with p = 1 under the power rule unless the observed count meets it); its confirmatory use is the live winter trial (§11, separate amendment). Pre-specified sensitivity: R² ≥ 0.8 (80 sites), reported, never substituted. Daily-tier onsets for H1/H4/H5b (computed 2026-11-08 by `scripts/eval/sonde_onset_counts.py`, run `sentinel_runs/sonde/ew_onset_counts_trainval`, registry experiment `ew_v2_onset_counts`; train and val blocks, train- and val-role stream sites only, Approved values; **test-role sites and test blocks never loaded**):

**Power projection for Test-A (stated assumption, no test values used):** the split report (`sonde_split_report.json`, written at split creation from site metadata) lists DO-min sites per role as train 450 / val 83 / test 236. Scaling the val-block onset rate at train-role sites (84 onsets over 2 WY at 325 observed of 450 listed sites) to the 236 test-role sites gives ≈ 44 H1 (< 3 mg/L) onsets in WY2024–25 (tier < 4: ≈ 63; tier < 2: ≈ 30). **H1 (< 3 mg/L) is therefore expected to be underpowered on Test-A alone (≈ 44 projected onsets < 50)**; the observed Test-A count is reported whatever it is (≥ 50 → claim allowed; otherwise "underpowered"). **Basin groups (W12):** site metadata (USGS DV series catalog, no values) lists 264 test-role DO sites in **28 basin groups**; in the val block onsets occurred in 15 of 42 train-role basin groups, so ≈ 10 test-role basin groups are expected to carry H1 onsets — the two-stage CI will be wide. H4 (≈ 5 projected; 38 test-role nitrate sites) and H5b (0 val, 3 test-role stream chl sites) are **expected to be underpowered** for Test-A and are reported descriptively; H5b is evaluated on lake/challenge tracks only. **Pre-declared secondary H1 analysis (lead decision 2026-11-08, before freeze): pooled Test-A ∪ Test-B (WY2024–2026).** Same frozen models, thresholds, test-role sites, endpoint (BSS vs identically recalibrated climatology at h = 3 d, basin-group / site-month two-stage bootstrap) and ≥ 50-onset rule as the primary; Approved values only: the WY2026 data are retrieved (USGS Water Data OGC API) **on or after the approval cut-off 2027-06-30** and scored once (pass `test_ab`, own single-pass marker; the code refuses to run before the cut-off). WY2026 label days still provisional at retrieval are treated as missing like all provisional values; because approval is not missing at random, the fraction of provisional WY2026 label days per site is reported. Test-B issue dates start after the 42-day embargo from 2025-10-01 (block definition), the pooled rows are the union of the Test-A and Test-B issue rows, and the test-role population, models and thresholds are those of the primary. A version on provisional WY2026 data may be reported **descriptively only**. **Data pin of the pooled pass (re-review W2, rule fixed now):** the 2027 retrieval is built into new dated snapshot directories and promoted like the 2026-11-08 refresh; before scoring, a dated record `docs/preregistration/FREEZE_ew_test_ab_data.json` (created on or after 2027-06-30; `FREEZE_ew.json` is never rewritten for this pass) lists `data_snapshots` for all four loader roles in the §6 format (manifest + content hash) and an `identity_check` of all rows ≤ 2023-09-30 against the Test-A pins (`identical` must be true — train/val inputs, climatology and models stay as frozen; otherwise the pass is refused and a new amendment is required). **Test-A rows of the pooled pass are re-derived from the 2027 snapshot** (one snapshot for the whole pooled pass); the number of WY2024–25 label days whose value or approval status changed vs the Test-A snapshot is reported. The code refuses the `test_ab` pass without that record or before the cut-off. The primary endpoint (Test-A alone) is unchanged and is never replaced by the pooled analysis; the pooled result is labelled secondary in every report.

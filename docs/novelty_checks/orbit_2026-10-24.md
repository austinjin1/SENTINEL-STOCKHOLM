# Novelty and literature check — SENTINEL-Orbit (M2), 2026-10-24

**Scope.** Satellite (Sentinel-2) pixels → in-situ chl-a / TSS / turbidity / Secchi, evaluated on held-out
lakes in held-out years, against a retrained MDN. This file supplements docs/research/02 §a–§c
(searched 2026-10-22).

## Searches (web, 2026-10-24 to 2026-10-25)
1. "Sentinel-2 inland lake chlorophyll-a retrieval matchups held-out lakes deep learning 2025 2026
   mixture density network AquaMatch" (extended search).
   - **Sherf et al., arXiv:2510.24124.** Residual DL with ML water classification on AquaMatch. Already
     in docs/research/02. Its lake-level split is used for XGBoost only; the DL model uses a random split.
   - **arXiv:2610.10496** (Oct 2026). Evolutionary architecture search for **bloom classification**
     (binary, AUC 0.82): 1,151 Baltic S2 matchups with lake-grouped held-out lakes. Not years; no
     GBM or MDN baseline; no regression.
   - **Salls et al. 2024, Remote Sens. 16(11)** (PMC11235139). MCI/NDCI on S2 in 103 US lakes, with a
     random 80/20 split. MCI on ρt gives multiplicative MAE 2.08 and MAPE ≈ 101%. **MCI was added to
     our index baselines** because of this paper.
   - Other hits use random splits, single regions, or SAR+optical CNNs (Water 17:1718, 2025).
2. "AquaCorr-Global matchup dataset 2026 MDN Sentinel-2 inland water quality benchmark". No paper
   using AquaCorr-Global was indexed. Related: Pahlevan et al. 2022 (MDN simultaneous); a Mendeley
   S2 chl-a matchup dataset (2026-06); a Lake Chapala matchup set; Lehmann/UFZ 2024 (German lakes,
   matchup-window study).

## Conclusion (provisional)
- We found no published study evaluating S2 pixels → in-situ WQ on **unseen lakes × unseen years**
  against a retrained MDN and against location/season baselines.
- The first-in-class framing remains open.
- A "SOTA" claim still requires beating MDN_B (retrained), the official MDN weights, GBM imagery+context
  and location kNN, with paired CIs excluding 0 (orbit_protocol.md §1).
- Re-check Scholar/Scopus before any paper text.

## Addendum (2026-10-24 ~23:00), search "Sentinel-2 water quality retrieval transformer or foundation model embeddings inland lakes 2025 2026 outperform XGBoost spatial cross-validation"
- **"A Spatiotemporal Transformer for Satellite Retrieval of Lake Turbidity"** (NeurIPS 2026 abstract page,
  neurips.cc/virtual/2026/174697). Accuracy drops sharply when whole lakes or regions are held out. With
  lat/lon as input, the transformer learns a location-indexed correction that collapses on another
  continent. Only the abstract was read. This supports our context dropout, the imagery-only variant
  and the unseen-lake headline.
- **ICML 2026 slides (icml.cc/media/icml-2026/Slides/76520.pdf).** Under leave-region-out splits, RF and
  XGBoost behave almost identically for chl-a, TSS and CDOM, and the authors conclude that data, not
  architecture, is the bottleneck. These are slides, not a full paper.
- **arXiv:2605.24515** ("Lake Detection and Water Quality Estimation in Sentinel-2 Data"). Not read yet.
- No paper was found showing a deep or foundation-model approach that beats XGBoost under held-out-lake
  splits for inland S2 retrieval.

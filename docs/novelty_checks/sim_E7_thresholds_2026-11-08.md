# Novelty re-check: E7 / S-T, national (NRSA) and UK replication of the EPA 300 µS/cm benchmark, 2026-11-08

**Claim checked (sim_protocol.md §8).** We apply three methods to NRSA 2008–2024 wadeable benthic samples and lab
SC:
- XC95/HC05 (EPA 2011 / Cormier & Suter 2013);
- TITAN-style change points;
- a richness-LOWESS baseline.

Strata are Central Appalachia (positive control vs 300 µS/cm), national, each ag_eco9, and England (EA, family
level). The claim label is **replication** (low novelty). This extends sim_E7_thresholds_2026-10-25.md.

**Method caveat.** Searches used the agent's general web index (WebSearch/WebFetch), not Scholar, Scopus or WoS.
Forward citations of Cormier & Suter 2013 and EPA 2011 were *approximated* by topical queries. The Environment
Agency and state grey literature may be poorly indexed.

## Effect on novelty wording
**Unchanged.** The study stays an independent **replication** with methodological triangulation. The permitted
wording from sim_E7_thresholds_2026-10-25.md stands: "an independent replication of the EPA field-based
conductivity benchmark using NRSA data, with methodological triangulation."

"Novel" may still be used only for the England (EA, family-level) application, and only if it remains unreported
after a Scholar/Scopus check. The 2016–2018 state critiques (Minnesota, Tennessee) should be cited as prior doubts
about transferability, not as peer-reviewed replications.

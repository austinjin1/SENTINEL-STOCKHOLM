**Claim checked (sim_protocol.md §9 E3).**
- Sites are classified by annual air–water A_r and Δφ with the Hare et al. 2021 thresholds (dam > 40 d excluded;

## New relevant items
**None new** that link Hare-type groundwater classes, or any multi-site groundwater index, to DO, DO_min
sensitivity or hypoxia.

New context items:
- **Parra-Suarez, Wild, Gilfedder, Geist, Barth, Frei & Peiffer 2025, *Water Research* 279:123368,
  doi:10.1016/j.watres.2025.123368**, "Subsurface Fe(II) affects concentrations of dissolved O2 in streamwater".
  - Citation verified on cris.fau.de.
  - A flume experiment in which Fe(II) inflow lowered pore- and open-water DO and accounted for about 17–21 % of
    O2 uptake (abstract-level).
  - **Supports the two-sided E3 hypothesis**, since groundwater can lower DO.
- **Lapides, Zipper & Hammond 2026, *Hydrol. Process.*, doi:10.1002/hyp.70444.** Groundwater pumping and stream
  temperature at 46 CONUS gages; impacts are largest where groundwater is cold and BFI > 50 %. Temperature only;
  Hare classes are not used (checked on the author page).
- **van Hamel, Janzing [first name U] & Brunner 2026, *Commun. Earth Environ.* 7:296,
  doi:10.1038/s43247-026-03269-6.** River heatwaves in 275 Alpine catchments are modulated by discharge and
  meltwater. Temperature only. The author list comes from the HydroShare data page; the journal page needs login.
- Context only:
  - [U] *Sustainability* 18(17):9127 (2026, SE Spain), groundwater input → thermal buffering;
  - a 2026 urban spring-fed streams study (newsletter-level) [U];
  - EPA CADDIS DO page, which notes groundwater inflow is often low in DO.

# Novelty and literature check: SENTINEL-Sim model (Twin v2 DO head), 2026-10-25

**Scope.** The architecture in docs/research/06 §f.3:
- a multi-site LSTM whose DO head outputs only daily GPP, ER and K600;
- these are integrated hourly through a fixed, differentiable single-station O2 mass balance (Benson–Krause DOsat, Schmidt-scaled K);
- trained on continuous USGS DO sensors at about 400–500 sites;
- plus metamorphic and counterfactual tests under warming.

**Method caveat.** Searches used the agent web-search tool (a general web index), not Google Scholar or Web of
Science, and no forward-citation tool was available. "Not found" means not found in this index.

## Searches (web, 2026-10-25)
1. "differentiable stream metabolism LSTM GPP ER K600 dissolved oxygen" (extended). No DL hit. Only classical
   inversion appeared: Appling et al. 2018 (JGR-B, doi:10.1002/2017JG004140), BASE, a coupled O2–CO2 model, and
   time-varying K600 from high-frequency DO (ScienceDirect S0013935124018449).
2. "physics-guided deep learning dissolved oxygen river metabolism hybrid model" (extended).
   - An et al. 2026, J. Hydrol. (S0022169426007730): physics-informed hybrid for riverine DO. Péclet and Damköhler
     numbers are used as priors. This is a 1-D advection–diffusion–reaction prior, not a metabolism head.
   - KGCatBoost 2024, J. Hydrol. (S0022169424006929): 15 Yangtze stations.
   - Other hits are decomposition + LSTM/TCN hybrids with no physics.
3. "hybrid process-based machine learning dissolved oxygen rivers CONUS multi-site". **Closest prior work:**
   - **Sadler, Koenig, Gorski, Carter & Hall Jr 2024, Hydrol. Process. 38(9):e15270, doi:10.1002/hyp.15270**
     (pubs.usgs.gov/publication/70259292). Two process-guided DL models predict **daily min, mean and max DO**
     in Delaware River Basin rivers. The abstract says the models also "predicted stream metabolism and gas exchange
     rates" in a multi-task setup.
   - The data release (doi:10.5066/P13T6EYN) covers **10 sites**, daily time step, NWIS DO targets, with Appling 2018
     metabolism estimates among the inputs.
   - Result: no gain over baselines on temporal or similar-site hold-outs; one approach helped at *dissimilar*
     sites. The authors suggest "more constrained" process guidance.
   - Full text not readable (Wiley 403), so we could not verify whether DO is computed *through* an integrated
     O2 mass balance or whether metabolism is only an auxiliary task [U].
   - Also found: DynQual + random-forest residual correction for global DO (EGU26-12540, abstract only).
4. "neural network inverse stream metabolism estimation GPP ER from dissolved oxygen time series". No NN
   inverse-metabolism hit. Only ROM-TM (δ18O inverse model), Nifong et al. (Freshw. Sci.), and coastal ANN
   GPP:R regressions.
5. "machine learning stream metabolism GPP ER prediction LSTM continental rivers" (extended).
   - **Guan, Shi, Woolway, Qin, Zhang & Ran 2025, ES&T 59(51):27800–27810, doi:10.1021/acs.est.5c05623.** DL
     reconstruction of daily metabolism at **293 US rivers**, 1980–2020, with projections (GPP +0.045, ER −0.078
     g O2 m⁻² d⁻¹ per decade; NEP declining). Read via the EGU26-12035 abstract. The architecture and training
     targets are not stated [U]; they are presumably streamMetabolizer estimates, i.e. metabolism regressed on
     drivers rather than DO fitted through physics.
   - Maavara et al. (Science, "A west of extremes"; Dryad doi:10.5061/dryad.c2fqz61kx): random forest for CONUS
     GPP/ER trained on Appling-derived estimates.
6. "Zhi LSTM dissolved oxygen … CAMELS-chem 2021". Zhi et al. 2021, ES&T 55:2357–2368: 236 basins, grab samples,
   LSTM "learns" solubility and misses peaks and troughs.
7. "Rahmani 2023 differentiable stream temperature structural priors". Rahmani, Appling, Feng, Lawson & Shen 2023,
   WRR 59(12):e2023WR034420, doi:10.1029/2023WR034420. Hybrid differentiable stream-T model on 415 basins.
   Source fractions "may not be adequately constrained" by one target. This is the closest *design* analogue,
   but for temperature, not DO.
8. "LSTM high-frequency dissolved oxygen sensor USGS hundreds of sites daily minimum DO". No hit at hundreds of
   sensor sites. Hits: Sadler 2024 (10 sites); a 2-station hourly LSTM; Upper Klamath Lake BRT/NN (4 sites).
9. "streamMetabolizer machine learning random forest predict GPP ER K600". Appling 2018; Maavara RF; RiverMET
   (17 Illinois sites; pubs.usgs.gov/publication/70237968).
10. "neural ODE OR universal differential equation stream metabolism". None. The nearest are Eawag neural-ODE
    streamflow work and a mechanistic latent-biomass DO model (EPFL).
11. "physics-informed neural network stream metabolism … 2024–2026" (extended). None.
12. "differentiable model dissolved oxygen river … 2025" (extended) and "Shen group differentiable water
    quality 2025 2026". No differentiable DO model was found. Shen-group δ work found is streamflow only.
13. "metamorphic testing LSTM water quality warming counterfactual DO".
    - Reichert et al. 2024 (HESS 28:2505) does metamorphic testing for **discharge**, not WQ.
    - Xia et al. 2025, arXiv:2503.09947 (accepted *Nexus*): LSTM, DeepONet and Informer on 482 basins and 20 WQ
      variables. Robustness and attribution tests only; **no counterfactual or physics-consistency tests**.
14. Also seen: Regier et al. 2026 (SFS abstract 135678, PNNL). Knowledge-guided ML classifies DO dynamics as
    biologically or physically driven from DO+T alone, explicitly *without* estimating metabolism.

## Conclusion (provisional)
- **Not novel:** "an LSTM predicting DO across the US" (Zhi 2021/2023), "DL predicting daily min/mean/max DO with
  metabolism as an auxiliary task" (Sadler 2024, 10 sites), and "DL reconstruction of CONUS metabolism"
  (Guan 2025, 293 rivers).
- **Not found in this index:** a *multi-site, continental* LSTM whose DO head emits **only** GPP, ER and K600 and
  computes hourly DO **through** a fixed, differentiable O2 mass balance, trained end-to-end on continuous sensor
  DO, with K600–Q and ER-Arrhenius priors and checks of the latents against Powell Center/NEON.
- Classification: **partially novel (extension)** of Sadler et al. 2024 (process-guided DO DL) and of Rahmani et al.
  2023 (differentiable structural priors), scaled from 10 sites to hundreds.
- Cite Sadler 2024 prominently. It is the direct precedent, and its negative result (no gain in-distribution) is
  a risk we must report against.
- Metamorphic or counterfactual *WQ* testing under warming also looks unreported (only discharge precedents),
  so it is a **partially novel** evaluation contribution.
- Permitted wording: "To our knowledge, this is the first continental-scale differentiable stream-metabolism
  DO model trained on high-frequency sensor data. It extends process-guided DO deep learning (Sadler et al. 2024)
  from 10 Delaware Basin sites to N sites." Avoid "first physics-guided DO model" and "first ML metabolism model".
- Before claiming novelty: read the Sadler 2024 full text (does DO pass through an integrated mass balance?)
  and Guan 2025 methods, and run a Scholar forward-citation check of Sadler 2024 and Rahmani 2023.
